Hand-Controlled Robot

A robot that copies your hand, over Bluetooth.
role
Hardware · firmware
tools
Arduino · HC-05 · accelerometer
when
2022
context
Personal project
tags
robotics · embedded

One summer in high school I built a robot you steer with your hand. I made a wearable glove with an accelerometer that reads how your hand is tilted and moving, and sends that over Bluetooth (an HC-05 module) to a little robot running on a pair of Arduinos. The robot mirrors your hand in real time, so tilting your hand drives it around.

It was one of my earlier builds, and the big lesson was how much a good mentor speeds things up. Having someone experienced to ask got me through the tricky parts far faster than I would have alone.
