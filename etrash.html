E-TRASH Hovercraft

A hovercraft built from a bin of e-waste.
role
Hardware · rapid prototyping
when
Jan 2025
context
E-TRASH Hackathon
team
3 people
outcome
1st place
tags
competition · hardware

E-TRASH is a hackathon with one rule: you can only build from a bin of donated electronic waste. My team of three dug through it, found a pile of fans and a working space heater, and decided to build a hovercraft. We used the space heater's metal grille as the chassis, cut foam padding into a skirt around the bottom, wrapped the whole thing in a trash bag to trap the air, and pointed the fans down for lift.

The idea was the easy part. The real work was tuning, trimming the foam skirt over and over to get a clean air seal, and it paid off: the thing floated really well. We never got enough thrust to drive it forward, so it hovered in place more than it raced, but it hovered, and that was enough to take first place.
